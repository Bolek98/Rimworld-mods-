2.13.2
- Every hostile mechanoid or drone the colony could not build yet (no researched recipe), or whose faction is above the world level, is removed – from any faction (mechanoids, human factions like the Wolfein MegaCorp, mod factions), arriving in any way (raids, quests, quest sites, events, map generation), on any map. Needs "Mechanoids follow the world level".
- Mechanoids and drones already on your maps are checked once after loading a save.
- One message per kind of stopped threat instead of one per pawn.

World at Your Level 2.13.1
==========================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).
- Fix: quests you blocked, or quests above the world level (mech quests), could still come from other mods (race mods, quest generators) that bypass the storyteller – e.g. the Wolfein "Defector" quest with MegaCorp drone waves. They are now dropped whoever makes them.
- Hostile mechanoids and drones of a faction above the world level that arrive on your home map some other way (quest waves, mod events) are removed on arrival, like out-of-level threat causers. Works with "World mechanoids" on.
