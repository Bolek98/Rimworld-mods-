World at Your Level 2.13.0
==========================
- New: "Recommended settings" switch at the top of the settings, on by default for new players. The world simply grows with your research.
- While it is on, the detailed settings are locked (you can still browse them). Faction exemptions and raid type lists stay editable.
- Turn it off to customize – values that differ from recommended get an orange CHANGED mark, click it to restore one value, or use "Reset all to recommended".
- Recommended values update themselves when a new version improves them.
- Bigger settings window.
- New: rare advanced-tech raids (suggested by a player) – now and then a raid brings gear one tech level above your colony. Fewer raiders, and the gear stays as loot, so you can discover new tech early. Off by default – set the chance per raid in the settings; only when the raiders' faction has such gear.
- New: rare low-tech raids – now and then a raid with gear one level below yours, but more raiders. Off by default, adjustable.
- Existing settings are kept – an update never overwrites your values.

World at Your Level 2.12.3
==========================
- Fixed: locked gear of arriving visitors, traders and raiders piled up at the map edge when "Leave locked items on the ground" was enabled.
- Raiders now keep locked items in their inventory – they drop as loot when the raider is defeated.
- Visitors and traders no longer leave locked items behind.
- Only your own colonists drop locked items on the ground.
