=== Development history since the last Workshop release (test versions) ===
2.18.0
- New "Threats" tab: switch off any threat or bad event (big, small, diseases, fallout, mod monsters …), or with "show all" any event at all. Turned off = it never starts, not even from quests or mods.
- Bosses you call yourself with a mechanitor (Diabolus, War Queen, Apocriton …) are no longer removed by "Also limit mechanoids" – they used to vanish right after arriving.
- The Threats tab also lists what this mod removes by itself (mechanoids and drones you have not researched – e.g. Wolfein drones –, weather machines above the world level, turrets better than yours) with the reason; tick one to keep it anyway.
- The "Raids" tab keeps raid strategies, arrival modes and random quests; threat events moved to the new tab (your choices stay).

2.17.0
- Factions have their own level (Evolution tab, on): each faction moves around the world level at its own pace – some lag behind, a few lead one level ahead. Their raiders and visitors carry gear of the faction's level. Destroying one of their settlements sets them back, trading helps them. The World tab lists every faction with its level and trend.
- Study foreign tech: a weapon or apparel above your research has a "Study" button – it is taken apart and gives research to the project that unlocks it. Needs a research bench.
- A new era brings a trader: a day or two after the world level rises, a caravan with goods of the new era arrives.
- Raids by real defence (Evolution tab, off by default): raid strength also follows your fighters, their gear and skills, turrets and combat mechanoids, not only colony wealth.

2.16.0
- Raiders' implants follow the world level (Arrivals tab, on by default): hostile pawns no longer get bionic, archotech or other implants above the world level. They are simply not installed, so nobody arrives with a missing arm instead.
- Raiders' drugs follow the world level (Arrivals tab, on by default): go-juice, yayo and other drugs above the world level are taken from hostile pawns' pockets as they are made, so they can't take them before the fight.
- Research cost by world level (World tab, off by default): research above the world level costs more (default +50 %), research below it less (default −25 %). The research screen shows the changed cost.

2.15.2
- Colony tier: Anomaly research (studied through knowledge, not at a bench) and research without a tech level no longer count, so the tier can't get stuck on projects most colonies never do. The level bar and "What the next tier brings" count the same way.
- A letter when the world level rises, saying what it means (settings, on by default; switch it off to get the short message as before). Drops still show a message.

2.15.1
- World tab: "What the next tier brings" – how many research projects are still missing for the next tier, the world level it leads to, and what arrives with it (new weapon and armor kinds, factions that start raiding, mechanoids, events, trader goods).
- Gradual change of era (Arrivals tab, off by default): after the world level rises, at first only about 1 in 5 arrivals carries gear of the new era; the share grows evenly over the set number of days (default 5). Which pawns lag is fixed per pawn; saved with the game.
- Fewer gaps: the gear filter now runs after other mods' pawn generation and spawn patches (races, kind extensions, sidearm mods), so gear they hand out afterwards no longer slips through.

2.15.0
- Why raiders have their gear: select an enemy (or a visitor) and the inspect pane shows what this mod took from them and what they got instead ("assault rifle → bolt-action rifle"). The raid message also names the most common swaps.

2.14.2
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

2.14.1
- Settings: new "Report a bug" button (bottom left) – a short report for a Workshop comment and a full one for GitHub Issues, with this mod's errors, the mod list and other mods that change the same game code. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.

=== Workshop release 2.14.0 (23 Oct 2026) – everything since the previous Workshop version ===
2.14.0
- New: "Strict: only what you crafted, also when matching the colony" – enemies may only carry weapons and apparel your colony has actually crafted (no makeshift rifles while you still use bows).
- New: "Enemy turrets only up to your own" – turrets of other factions (quest sites, bases, sieges) are removed unless you have built a turret of the same tech level yourself.
- Every hostile mechanoid or drone the colony could not build yet, or whose faction is above the world level, is removed – from any faction (also human and mod factions), arriving in any way (raids, quests, quest sites, events, map generation), on any map. Needs "Mechanoids follow the world level".
- Mechanoids and drones already on your maps are checked once after loading a save.
- One message per kind of stopped threat instead of one per pawn.
- Settings: the CHANGED marks and frames show properly on RimWorld 1.7.

World at Your Level 2.13.1
==========================

- Fix: quests you blocked, or quests above the world level (mech quests), could still come from other mods (race mods, quest generators) that bypass the storyteller – e.g. the Wolfein "Defector" quest with MegaCorp drone waves. They are now dropped whoever makes them.
