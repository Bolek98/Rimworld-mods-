=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
3.1.0
- Clean-up after a battle: one button in the Hauling overview allows the weapons, apparel and bodies enemies left around the base (the game forbids them) and has them hauled before everything else. A message tells you when a battle is over; optionally it cleans up by itself.

3.0.4
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

3.0.3
- Settings: new "Report a bug" button (bottom left) – a short report for a Workshop comment and a full one for GitHub Issues, with this mod's errors, the mod list and other mods that change the same game code. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.

3.0.2
- Settings: the CHANGED marks and frames show properly on RimWorld 1.7 (they were solid boxes).

Haul Smarter 3.0.1
==================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).
- Fix: things stayed in the pockets for too long, so cooks had no ingredients and nobody brought meals to patients or prisoners.
  Food, medicine and drugs now go to storage within about 15 in-game minutes, everything else within an hour (after the current job;
  harvesting and mining still fill the pockets first).
- "Things on the way" never takes food, medicine or drugs any more.
