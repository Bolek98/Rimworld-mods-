=== Workshop release 1.0.0 (30 Oct 2026) ===
1.0.0
First Workshop release.
- Steward add-on: the colony reacts to fires, raids, wounded colonists, hunger, butchering and harvest on its own; safe area during raids.

První vydání na Workshopu.
- Doplněk Stewarda: kolonie sama reaguje na požár, přepadení, zraněné, hlad, porcování a sklizeň; bezpečná oblast při přepadení.

=== Development history before the first Workshop release (test versions) ===
1.0.8
- Safe area during raids: the colony's work mechs, robots and droids go to safety too (fighting mechs stay out to fight), and whoever is outside stops the job and goes in at once.

1.0.7
- Medical emergency: for a colonist lying hurt only rescue or tending is started – never another doctor job from a mod (giving drugs …). A colonist bleeding fast on the floor is tended (stabilised) first and then carried to bed; a slow bleeder is carried to bed first.

1.0.6
- The game no longer warns at start that the Steward core dependency has no download link.

1.0.5
- Fix: the "Storage full" situation failed with an error with storage mods that expect a hauler (Deep Storage and others); one odd item no longer stops the count.

1.0.4
- Settings: the lists of crises and situations were empty – the list was taller than its scroll area and the rest went into an invisible second column; now it is one scrollable list

1.0.3
- Crises no longer lower or re-plan urgent work (firefighting, urgent hauling, rescue)
- Settings: the lists of crises and situations could stay empty (one text failing stopped the whole list) – each line is now drawn on its own, a failing text is written to the log
