=== Workshop release 1.2.0 (13 Nov 2026) ===
1.2.0
New
- Research gets done: while a project is chosen, the best researcher has research on 2 (on 1 from 5 colonists) and his hauling and cleaning one step lower. Research is the last column, so before it never came first. Can be switched off.
- New "Skills" tab in the settings: for each work, from which skill a colonist may do it at all and from which skill he gets it as a backup. The best colonist always keeps the work.
- Who has priority: when two parts of Steward want different things for one cell, the stronger one wins (lives, your rules, roles, priority areas, crises … base plan). The order can be changed in the settings, and the cell's tooltip shows who wanted what.
- Works with modded plant growers (hydroponics, planters …) and modded work benches; new "Waiting work – Check now" button shows what Steward sees waiting, per work type.
- "Top job – skill first" (60 %): a colonist's own best skill counts more than the colony's current needs.

Fixes
- Hunting is no longer taken away from colonists with a low animal gather yield.
- Weak backups are allowed again for most work (a weak backup is better than nobody); the strict limits can still be switched on.

Nové
- Výzkum se dělá: dokud je vybraný projekt, nejlepší výzkumník má výzkum na 2 (od 5 kolonistů na 1) a nošení a úklid o stupeň níž. Výzkum je poslední sloupec, takže dřív nikdy nepřišel na řadu. Jde vypnout.
- Nová záložka „Dovednosti“ v nastavení: u každé práce od jaké dovednosti ji kolonista smí dělat vůbec a od jaké jako zálohu. Nejlepší kolonista práci vždy dostane.
- Kdo má přednost: když dvě části Stewarda chtějí v jedné buňce něco jiného, vyhraje ta silnější (životy, tvoje pravidla, role, prioritní oblasti, krize … základní plán). Pořadí jde změnit v nastavení a tooltip buňky ukáže, kdo co chtěl.
- Funguje s pěstírnami z modů (hydroponie, truhlíky …) i pracovními stoly z modů; nové tlačítko „Čekající práce – zkontrolovat“ ukáže, co Steward vidí čekat, po druzích práce.
- „Hlavní práce – nejdřív dovednost“ (60 %): kolonistova nejlepší dovednost váží víc než aktuální potřeby kolonie.

Opravy
- Lov se už nebere kolonistům s nízkým výnosem ze zvířat.
- Slabá záloha je u většiny práce zase povolená (slabá záloha je lepší než nikdo); přísné limity jde zapnout.

=== Workshop release 1.1.0 (10 Nov 2026) ===
1.1.0
New
- Small colonies (up to 6 colonists): the work that matters most now stays on 2, the next on 3, the rest last – no more "everything on 2".
- Calmer: outside real danger a new plan comes at most every 6 in-game hours – no constant reshuffling at high speed.
- Priority areas win: colonists in a priority area keep that area's work switched on.
- Skilled backup only from skill 6 (setting) for skilled work; surgery left to doctors from Medicine 8 when one is available. Simple work stays with everybody.
- Works with Steward – Work Split.

Fixes
- Known colonists are kept across loading (no more newcomer window after every load).
- The game's priority hints in the Work tab are no longer cut off.

Nové
- Malé kolonie (do 6 kolonistů): nejdůležitější práce zůstanou na 2, další na 3, zbytek na konci – už ne „všechno na 2“.
- Klidnější: mimo skutečné nebezpečí přijde nový plán nejvýš jednou za 6 herních hodin – žádné neustálé přehazování při vysoké rychlosti.
- Prioritní oblasti vyhrávají: kolonisté v prioritní oblasti mají práci té oblasti vždy zapnutou.
- Záloha na odborné práce jen od dovednosti 6 (nastavení); operace nechá lékařům od Medicíny 8, když nějaký je. Jednoduchá práce zůstává všem.
- Funguje se Steward – Work Split.

Opravy
- Známí kolonisté zůstanou i po načtení (už žádné okno nováčků po každém načtení).
- Herní nápovědy priorit na kartě Práce už nejsou useknuté.

=== Development history since the last Workshop release (test versions) ===
1.1.5
- Colonists working in a priority area keep that area's work switched on, so crises and styles can no longer pull them away from it. Add-ons can put their own button in the Work tab (used by Crises).
- Kolonisté v prioritní oblasti mají práci té oblasti vždy zapnutou, takže je krize ani styl kolonie už od ní neodvedou. Doplňky mohou mít vlastní tlačítko na kartě Práce (používají Krize).

1.1.4
- Small colony (up to 6 colonists): the work that matters most now stays on 2, the next on 3, the rest last – before, almost everything was on 2 and the game's column order decided (wood came last).
- Malá kolonie (do 6 kolonistů): práce, na které teď nejvíc záleží, zůstane na 2, další na 3, zbytek nakonec – dřív bylo skoro všechno na 2 a rozhodovalo pořadí sloupců ve hře (dřevo přišlo na řadu poslední).
- Calmer: a new plan for anything but real danger comes at most every 6 in-game hours (was 2) – no more constant reshuffling at high speed.
- Klidnější: nový plán kvůli čemukoli kromě skutečného nebezpečí nejvýš jednou za 6 herních hodin (dřív 2) – při vysoké rychlosti už se priority pořád nepřehazují.
- New situation: new colony (start plan) – used by Crises & Situations.
- Nová situace: nová kolonie (startovní plán) – používá ji Krize a situace.

1.1.3
- Known colonists are kept across loading (after every load the whole colony counted as newcomers and the newcomer window came up). Surgery guard and work matching follow Work Split's own surgery work and keep split parts with their own parent.
- Známí kolonisté se po načtení hry pamatují (po každém načtení se celá kolonie brala jako nováčci a vyskočilo okno nováčků). Hlídání operací a párování prací počítá s vlastní prací operace z Work Split a menší práce drží jen u jejich původní práce.

1.1.2
- Works with Steward – Work Split: its smaller work types count as the work they came from (always someone on surgery and butchering, work styles, priority areas)
- Spolupráce se Steward – Work Split: jeho menší práce se počítají jako práce, ze které vznikly (vždy někdo na operacích a řeznictví, styly práce, oblasti priorit)

1.1.1
- Minimum skill for backups now only applies to crafting, art, construction and research – doctoring, wardens, childcare, cooking, plants, mining and animals stay with every backup again (colonists stood around with nothing to do). A colonist with nothing to do gets waiting work even below the minimum skill.
- Minimální dovednost pro zálohy platí už jen pro výrobu, umění, stavění a výzkum – doktořina, dozor, péče o děti, vaření, rostliny, těžba a zvířata zůstávají zase všem zálohám (kolonisté postávali bez práce). Kolonista bez práce dostane čekající práci i pod minimální dovedností.

1.1.0
- Skilled backup only for skilled colonists: doctoring, research, crafting, art, cooking and other skilled work stays as backup only with colonists from skill 6 (setting, 0 = everybody); simple work like hauling and cleaning stays with everybody. Surgery is left to doctors from Medicine 8 when one is on the map and assigned (setting, 0 = off); weaker doctors still tend. Forced orders and emergencies are never blocked.
- Odborná záloha jen pro zkušené: doktořinu, výzkum, výrobu, umění, vaření a další odborné práce mají jako zálohu jen kolonisté od dovednosti 6 (nastavení, 0 = všichni); jednoduché práce jako nošení a úklid zůstávají všem. Operace dělají lékaři od Medicíny 8, když je takový na mapě a má doktořinu (nastavení, 0 = vypnuto); slabší lékaři dál ošetřují. Přímé rozkazy a nouze se nikdy neblokují.

- Záložka Práce: herní nápovědy priority už nejsou useknuté vedle tlačítek Stewarda (např. „low“ v čínštině); znovu se vykreslí jen tam, kam se vejdou.

=== Development history before the first Workshop release (test versions) ===
3.4.1
- Keep zone: "Bring now" (on the zone and in the "What and how much" window) – free colonists drop what they are doing and carry the missing things to the zone right away, one load each; the rest follows with normal hauling. Drafted, sleeping colonists and those fighting fires, treating or rescuing are left alone.

3.4.0
- Keep zone: new "What and how much" window – pick things from a list (search, only things you have) and set an amount for each (empty = everything). Colonists bring each thing until the zone holds the amount and a hauler carries only what is missing. Only listed things are kept; anything else is hauled away.
- The "Keep zone here" button on an orbital trade beacon opens this window right away.

3.3.1
- Keep zone: orbital trade beacons get a "Keep zone here" button that covers the beacon's whole trade area. Traders still see and buy everything in it; only colonists leave it alone.
- Keep zone: only things the zone is set to keep are locked – goods dropped by a trade ship or anything else that lands there is hauled away as usual.

3.3.0
- New: Keep zone (Zones menu). A stockpile where you pick what colonists bring in (storage tab, starts empty); while it is locked nobody takes anything out of it – not to eat, craft, build or equip, and nothing is hauled elsewhere. A "Locked" button on the zone turns it into a normal stockpile when you need something.

3.2.5
- Faster: the ★ marks over priority workbenches are drawn only once per frame and only for benches on the screen (they took about 6 ms every second).

3.2.4
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

3.2.3
- Fix: counting items to haul in a priority area could fail with storage mods that expect a hauler (Deep Storage and others).

3.2.2
- Settings: a long settings page no longer continues in an invisible second column

3.2.1
- Crises no longer lower or re-plan urgent work (firefighting, urgent hauling, rescue) – during a raid urgent hauling went to the lowest priority
- Crisis rules for a work no longer pull in unrelated modded work types of the same skill (a raid raised diplomacy, waiting tables and shrine care together with warden work)
- Loading a priority set no longer fails when a colonist is in two lists (slave and colonist)

3.2.0
- Copy priorities: middle-click a colonist's name in the Work tab → "Copy priorities to…" one colonist or everybody else (shown first, locks respected)
- Priority sets: the new "Sets" button saves the current priorities under a name (Winter, Defence, Harvest …) and loads them again later – loading shows the changes first
- A new colonist joins while the steward is off: a window offers the work the steward would give him (setting "Offer work for a new colonist")
- Weekly summary: once a week a short message and history line – plans and changes, new colonists, work that waited without anybody, crises (can be turned off)
- Apprentices (off by default): a colonist with a passion and little skill learns a work on level 3 next to a master
- What's new: after an update of Bolek98's mods a window shows once what changed since the version you played (all mods in one window, can be turned off)

3.1.0

- Locks are easy to see: a "Locks (N)" button in the Work tab when anything is locked, with a list of locked colonists, columns and cells and unlocking one by one or all at once; a locked colonist's name is highlighted
- Hovering a colonist's name in the Work tab tells why the steward plans him or not (locked, forced work from Achtung!, down, slave, guest, baby, another map) and which locks and health limits apply to him

3.0.5
- Work no longer swaps back and forth between two colonists on every plan: whoever already does a work keeps it unless somebody is clearly better, equal colonists always end in the same order
- A colonist could end up without any top job (priority 1) during a crisis or a focus – fixed
- A downed, anaesthetized or blinded colonist no longer loses all his work; it stays as it was until he is up again
- Keyz' Allow Utilities urgent hauling and finishing off are treated as urgent work (like firefighting)
- Crisis rules for hauling no longer pull in unrelated no-skill work types (priority cleaning, power generation, recycling …)
- If the base plan fails, the priorities stay untouched instead of a half-empty plan being written
- The log lists up to 60 changes per plan and says how many more there were

3.0.4
