- First version: guard duty for combat mechs (patrol of the home area edge, outer doors and valuable rooms, map sweeps, intercepting enemies, holding the attacked side, recharging one at a time, leaving the fight when badly damaged), a "Duty" button on every machine (Auto, Guard, Work, Rest), automatic mech repair, droids that never sleep work around the clock, droids take firefighting first, warnings (energy, reactor, neutroamine) and a "Droids" tab. Mechanoids, VRE androids and other robot races.

Mechanoidi, androidi z VRE a další robotické rasy.
