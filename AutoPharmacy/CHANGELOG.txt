1.4.0
- Addictions are kept fed: an addicted colonist gets his drug before withdrawal – luciferium early and always (setting, on)
- Mood: a mild non-addictive drug already near the minor break line, not only after it is worse (setting, on)
- Doctors carry two doses of the drugs given most often (only safe ones) and use what they carry first – no walk to the stockpile (setting, on)
- A message when a needed drug is missing or out of reach – at most once a day per drug; nothing is ordered by itself (setting, on)
- Sanguophages (Biotech) get a hemogen pack when hemogen runs low
- Drugs the colonist's ideology forbids are not given (an addiction still gets its dose)
- Settings: search field for the drug list and a History button – who got what, when and why (the last 60)
- What's new: after an update a window shows once what changed (together with Bolek98's other mods; can be turned off)
- Six new medicines with own art: herbal pain salve, fever tea and calming tea (neolithic, crafting spot or drug lab), coagulant powder, antitoxin injector and burn gel (industrial, drug lab, Medicine production). Doctors use them on their own like any other drug.
- Wounds: a drug that speeds up healing is given to colonists with many fresh wounds

1.3.5
- Settings: new "Report a bug" button (bottom left) – a short report for a Workshop comment and a full one for GitHub Issues, with this mod's errors, the mod list and other mods that change the same game code. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.
