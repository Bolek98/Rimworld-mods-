2.49.3
- Each priority area works in the colony style that fits its work: everything in Balanced (and your own style), building in Building, mining in Mining, plants in Fields and forest / Farming, hauling and cleaning in Tidy; rescue always. A waiting area shows which style it needs.
- A colonist who can do nothing in its own area helps in another working area.
- A new priority area can take colonists from older automatic areas.
- An area where nobody did anything for two days ends by itself.

2.48.0
- Mothers care for their babies: childcare on the top priority until the baby is a child; the mother's other top-priority work moves one step down.
- Nannies: babies without a mother who can care for them (and the third baby of one mother) get a nanny – the colonist best at it (social skill, passion, few top jobs), kept while needed. One nanny per two babies.
- New setting "Mothers care for their babies, nannies help" (on, needs Biotech).

2.47.0
- Automatic schedule adapts to every colonist: 1–5 hours of recreation before sleep, more while a colonist lacks recreation or the mood is low, fewer when they are fine (checked every 6 in-game hours).
- The hour after waking and the hour before recreation are "Anything" – hungry or bored colonists don't have to wait for the evening.
- If you change a colonist's schedule yourself, Steward leaves that colonist alone.
- Schedule care steps in earlier: close to a mental break or with recreation almost empty → recreation now.
- Hint when a colonist is bored of most recreation kinds (more time won't help – build other kinds).

Steward 2.46.2
==============
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).
- New settings tab "Messages & meals": "only important messages top left" and "eat at a table and use the toilet".
  These are your own choices – no mode (Simple / Recommended / Advanced) changes or locks them.
- "Only important messages" is now off by default (switch it on in the new tab).
