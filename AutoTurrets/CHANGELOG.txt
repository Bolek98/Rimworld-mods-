=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
1.5.0
- Turrets tab: every turret, mortar and gun (vanilla and mods) in one list. Ticked ones fire on their own; unticked manned ones need a colonist again, unticked automatic ones hold fire until you pick a target.
- Materials for every turret: each turret that is rearmed – also modded ones that need their own ammo or fuel – gets a "Materials…" button where you tick what else it may be rearmed with (metals, wood, stone blocks, ammo, other resources). Changes work right away, no restart.
- The material switches (any metal, wood, stone) now work right away too.
- Buttons on every turret of yours: "Set forced target" also on automatic turrets, "Auto rearm" on every turret that is rearmed, "Auto load shells" on mortars that fire alone, and "Ammo" – pick the one shell or material this turret uses (or anything allowed).
- New options: special shells (EMP, incendiary, firefoam) only for mortars you set to them; turrets that need their own ammo can take any metal too; turrets need no ammo at all (for modded turrets whose ammo you can't make).

1.4.0
- Turrets in walls shoot only outward (setting, on by default): a wall turret only fires at targets on the outer side of its wall – the side that is outdoors while the other side is a room. A turret in a wall between two rooms (or where no side is clearly outside) still shoots in every direction. Turrets on the floor are not affected.

1.3.1
- Smart shell choice (settings, on by default): an automatic mortar doesn't waste shells – EMP only at mechanoids and shielded enemies, incendiary not at mechanoids, firefoam never at enemies but at fires in the home area. Haulers bring EMP shells when most enemies are mechanoids, otherwise high-explosive ones (if the mortar's filter allows them).
- New option "Turrets don't hit our buildings" (off by default): bullets from your turrets fly past your own walls, sandbags and other buildings unless you aim at them, and their shell blasts don't damage them. Enemy buildings are hit as usual.
- Friendly fire protection now also covers modded shooting buildings of yours that aren't regular turrets.

1.3.0
- Turrets in walls look like part of the wall: a 1×1 wall turret is drawn as a piece of wall in its material (stone blocks, metal or wood – chosen when you place it, 5 extra), joined with the walls next to it, with the gun on top. Before, the old wall was taken down and the turret stood there on its normal base like on the floor. Bigger wall turrets keep their base.

1.2.2
- Settings in three tabs (Shooting, Ammo and materials, Turrets in walls). Fix: the turret list was drawn into an invisible second column, so it looked empty and the settings stuttered; now the list has its own scroll area and only the visible cards are drawn.

1.2.1
- Nicer settings page: sections, turret cards with icons, search, "Select all / Clear all", a counter, a confirmation after each button and a notice when a restart is needed.

1.2.0
- Turrets in walls (settings, off by default, after a restart): picked turrets get a second version "… (in wall)" that is built over a wall or smoothed rock wall and works as a wall. Small turrets (up to 2×2) are picked by default.

1.1.0
- Friendly fire switch (settings): when off, bullets from your turrets fly past your colonists, guests and allies, and blasts from their shells don't hurt them.
- Turrets that rearm with metal can optionally also be refilled with wood and/or stone blocks (settings, after a restart).
