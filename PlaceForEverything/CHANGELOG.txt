=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
0.1.2
- New button on every stockpile and shelf: stack limit (how full a cell may get with one kind of item) and refill threshold (a cell is topped up only once it drops below it). Hauling respects both; stored items are never moved out. Setting, on.

0.1.1
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

0.1.0
- First version: finds items that no stockpile or shelf accepts and gives each a logical place in your existing storage (freezer, hospital, kitchen, next to the workbench that uses it, outside, or where similar items lie). Preview with a choice per item, then apply; the last change can be undone. Default storage names get a label of what is inside. Alert when items have nowhere to go, and a button on every stockpile and shelf.
