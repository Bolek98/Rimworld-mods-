Gender Balance 1.3.0
====================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).

New settings window
- Four profile cards on top: Like the game (50/50), More women, More men, Custom.
  One click sets everything; Custom unlocks the sliders.
- Shows how many men and women your colony has right now (in a running game).
- Same look as my other mods: sections, CHANGED marks (click to go back to the game's value).
- Settings from older versions are kept (they open as Custom).

Unchanged
- Separate sliders for people joining your colony and for everyone else.
- Per-race sliders for Humanoid Alien Races.
- Old world pawns that the game re-uses follow the chosen ratio too.
- Never touches genders a quest, relative, pawn kind or one-gender race asks for.
