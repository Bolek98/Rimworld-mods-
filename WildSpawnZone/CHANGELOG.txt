Wild Spawn Zone 1.1.0
=====================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).

New: settings
- Recommended settings switch (on by default) – the mod works right away.
- Turn the zone effect on/off without erasing the painted zone.
- Never in the home area (was always on, now a setting).
- Birds may fly in anywhere (off by default).
- Amount of wild animals: 0–300 % (how many live on the map and how fast they come).
- Same settings look as my other mods.

Unchanged
- Paint the zone in the Zone menu; wild animals then appear only inside it.
- Events (migrations, manhunters, thrumbos …) are not changed.
