=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
2.3.2
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

2.3.1
- Faster start: the scan cache now really works – mod settings files (which many mods rewrite at every start) no longer count as a change, so the 25 s scan is skipped while mods and language stay the same.

2.3.0
- Mods (new tab): how much of each mod is translated, with a progress bar and a search. Untick a mod to keep it in English – its texts go back to the original and are not sent for translation.
- Export: a mod's translation (or all of them) is saved as a normal Languages folder (Keyed + DefInjected) – copy it into the mod or send it to the mod's author.

2.2.2
- Fix: names of mods are never translated (the mod settings list showed "Simple Sidearms" as a translated sentence).

2.2.1
- Editor: searching no longer makes the game stutter – the search runs in the background, only the rows you see are drawn, and the list does not rebuild itself every few seconds while machine translation runs. URT's own window is not live-translated.

2.2.0
- Editor (new tab): find any text by its original or translation and write your own version; go back to the machine version, keep the English original or translate again. Filters: all, mine, problems, untranslated. Your own texts are never replaced by the machine.
- My words: a word you set ("kolonista" → "osadník") is used in every translation – whole words, the first letter's case kept.
- Live translation collects less junk: texts cut by the game ("Mod Sort Manage..."), version numbers, hashes and words of your own language ("Pacient", "Mramor") are not sent to translation any more, and a text the services could not translate is not asked again in the same session. A line in the log every 10 minutes tells how live translation goes.

2.1.0
- Stage 2 – texts made in code: messages, letters, buttons, tooltips and info lines that mods write directly in their code are now translated where they are drawn (on the screen only, the game data does not change). Known texts appear at once; new English texts are collected and translated in the background, the texts on your screen first. Numbers do not matter ("Harvested 25 rice" and "Harvested 3 rice" are one text). Names of colonists, factions, places and mods are never translated. Setting "Texts made in code (live)", on by default.
- Faster start: the result of the scan is kept on disk and used again while your mods, their files and the language stay the same (the 20+ second scan is skipped).
- Faster start: translations of plain def fields (labels, descriptions …) are put in directly instead of through the game's slower injection.
- What's new: after an update a window shows once what changed (together with Bolek98's other mods; can be turned off).

2.0.1
- Fix (important): 2.0.0 also translated identifiers that some mods keep in text fields (body part names, enum values, lists of work types, paths) – this broke Steward styles, RJW body parts and pawn graphics. Now only fields that hold text a player reads are translated (labels, descriptions, messages, grammar rules …).

2.0.0
- 2.0 test, stage 1: new clean translator. Free services without a key (Microsoft Edge, Yandex, Google, MyMemory) with automatic fallback; DeepL, Azure, Google Cloud, Gemini, OpenAI-compatible AI (also local Ollama/LM Studio) and LibreTranslate with your own key and a Test button.
- Translates def texts and Keyed texts through the game's own language system; every translation is checked (placeholders, grammar [tokens], wrong language, untranslated) – a broken one is never used.
- Grammar rules: only the text after -> is translated, tokens kept exactly; missing grammatical cases of languages like Czech (imageNom …) fall back to the plain keyword – no more ERR.
- Translation memory in a new compact format with a journal (no data loss on a crash); the 0.9 cache, manual corrections and keep-original list are taken over once and checked – broken entries are translated again.
- Not yet in this stage: live translation of texts that mods build in code, the untranslated-text collector, glossary and export (next stages).

0.9.57
- Fix: "ERR" on the world map and in quests. Grammar rule translations are only used when they keep exactly the original [tokens]; names already saved as ERR (settlements, factions, world features, landmarks, quest names) are generated again when a save loads.
