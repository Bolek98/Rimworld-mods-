0.3.2
- Settings: new "Report a bug" button (bottom left) – a short report for a Workshop comment and a full one for GitHub Issues, with this mod's errors, the mod list and other mods that change the same game code. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.

0.3.1
- Settings: the CHANGED marks and frames show properly on RimWorld 1.7 (they were solid boxes).

Research Fixer 0.2.0
====================
- New (optional, off by default): Mark newly researched things. With hundreds of mods it is hard to tell what a research just unlocked – now everything it unlocked gets a green NEW mark in the workbench "Add bill" menu and a green dot in the Architect menu. The mark disappears when you make or build the thing for the first time. Settings → Research Fixer → Extras.
- Yet Another Research Tree (YART) support: the Research Fixer button is now in YART's bottom bar (next to zoom and settings) – before it was only in the vanilla research window.
- If one patch fails (another mod or a game update), the rest of the mod keeps working.

Research Fixer 0.1.1
====================
- The Research Fixer button is now always visible in the research window (top right of the left panel), also with research UI mods.

Research Fixer 0.1.0
====================
- First release.
- Finds industrial and higher weapons, armor and buildings from mods that can be made without any research.
- Creates new research projects per mod and era in a new "Mod research" tab, connected to the right vanilla research.
- Finds things whose research is two or more eras below them and adds the right vanilla research.
- Nothing changes until you check the list and press Apply; every group and fix can be skipped; mod files are never changed.
- Button in the research window and in the mod settings.
- Works with World at Your Level and Smart Colonist Gear.
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (works on 1.6 too).
