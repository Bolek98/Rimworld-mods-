=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
0.3.0
- New call window: categories on the left (favorites, recently called, ships, caravans, and by what traders sell – weapons, medicine, food, tech …), trader cards with faction, relations, price and typical goods, search box.
- Detail of the chosen trader: everything it sells sorted by kind, and what it would buy from your stores with an estimate in silver.
- Special order: for an extra fee the trader brings extra goods of the kind you choose.
- Caravans: choose the side of the map they come from.
- Warning when enemies are on the map. Loyalty discount: every 5 calls 5 % off (max 20 %, can be turned off).

0.2.0
- You can call caravans of friendly factions too (every kind they send: bulk goods, combat supplier, slaver, exotic …). A caravan costs 60 % of the fee (setting).

0.1.1
- The called ship is now put in orbit directly. Before, mods that take over the orbital trader incident (Trader Ships, vehicle mods) could swallow a paid call.
- Letter when it arrives; a log warning if another mod removes the ship early.

0.1.0
- First version: call any orbital trader from the comms console, stock preview, normal or express delivery, pay from stored silver, alert countdown, retries for two days and refunds, called traders stay longer.
