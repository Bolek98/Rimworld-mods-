0.10.3

- Bills: a mine recipe added later in the game (Mines 2.0 custom resources) that still reads 'Mine for …' in English is shown as 'Mine for <product>' in your language.
- Zakázky: tři malá tlačítka u každé zakázky přepnou režim jedním klikem – × N krát, ≥ dokud nemáš X, ∞ donekonečna.

- Karta postavy: dovednosti ve stejném pořadí jako ve hře (Střelba první … Intelekt poslední); v hlavičce je za věkem i žena nebo muž.

0.10.1
- Health: Outerm (all three figures) – the arms now follow the real arms in front of the wings, so an injured or missing arm is shown in the right place.

0.10.0
- Health: own figures for humans (woman, man, child, fat, muscular, thin) and for 13 races – Wolfein, Miho, Maru, Yuran, Vivi, Saclean, Outerm, Nearmare, Idhale, Nivarian, Mugirl, Tailin, Mariponica – by sex and age, with their ears, tails, horns and wings drawn in. Body parts, organs and bones are fitted onto each figure. Other races keep the body-type figure with features drawn over it.

0.9.2
- Health: small R / L letters at the figure's feet – the figure faces you, so its right side is on your left.
- Operations: body parts you don't have in stock are listed greyed (new chip 'Part not in stock', and in All for a missing body part) with where to get them – the workbench, research still needed, or trade only. Click one to queue it at a workbench.

0.9.1
- The life log also notes heavy wounds, operations (who did them, and failed ones), recovering from diseases, deaths (and for their family and friends), royal titles, ideoligion roles, birthdays and finished research.

0.9.0
- New Character tab: a dossier (portrait, childhood and adulthood, traits, what they will not do, ideoligion, birthday) and three pages – Skills (bars with passion and progress), Life (a timeline by year: birth, joining the colony, the game's tales and our own life log: mental breaks, lost and new body parts, skill levels, partners, children) and Records.
- New Social tab: everyone they know with what each thinks of the other; click one to see why – every reason both ways, compatibility and romance chance. Without a choice: partner, best friend, worst rival and the last talks.
- Both tabs can be resized and switched back to the original with one click; both can be switched off in the settings.

0.8.0

- Own body pictures (no art from other mods) and the race features packed in one picture per body.

0.7.0
- Health tab: a new anatomy figure with three views – Body (each part coloured by its state: healthy, hurt, missing, prosthesis, bionic, archotech), Organs and Bones (broken bones red).
- Race features from the race itself (ears, tails, horns, wings, halo …) – also for races from mods.
- Point at a part to see what is wrong there, click to list only that part, right click for its operations.
- The ◐ button switches between the anatomy figure, the colonist's own silhouette and the portrait.

0.5.1
- Bills: the - and + buttons were hidden under the "…" button – now they sit next to the count, a bit bigger.

0.5.0
- Bills: - and + next to the count change "Do X times" or "Do until you have X" right in the list (Shift ±10, Ctrl ±100).
- Linked bills: "…" on a bill → "Link with a bill on another workbench" – bills with the same recipe share their settings (count, target, pause, materials, stockpiles); a change to one is copied to the others. Marked "linked ×N" with the other workbenches in the tooltip.
- Rename a bill from the "…" menu (the game's own bill name).
- "…" in the header: copy all bills of a workbench and paste them here or into several workbenches of the same kind at once (priorities come along).
- "Do until you have X" bills show when they also count worn/carried items or only one stockpile (set in Edit).

- Health details: the exact hit points of every body part next to its name (e.g. "Left leg 20/30"), and the time left for conditions whose label does not show it.
- "Operations planned: 2" in the health header when operations are planned – a click opens them.
- Safer drawing: a recipe without an icon shows a "?", a drawing error no longer leaves the tab with broken text or colours, and Esc in a search field just leaves the field.

0.4.1
