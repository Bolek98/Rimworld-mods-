=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
0.5.0
- Bills: - and + next to the count change "Do X times" or "Do until you have X" right in the list (Shift ±10, Ctrl ±100).
- Linked bills: "…" on a bill → "Link with a bill on another workbench" – bills with the same recipe share their settings (count, target, pause, materials, stockpiles); a change to one is copied to the others. Marked "linked ×N" with the other workbenches in the tooltip.
- Rename a bill from the "…" menu (the game's own bill name).
- "…" in the header: copy all bills of a workbench and paste them here or into several workbenches of the same kind at once (priorities come along).
- "Do until you have X" bills show when they also count worn/carried items or only one stockpile (set in Edit).

0.4.2
- Right click on a mark on the silhouette or on a condition: the operations for that body part in a menu, and "Show operations for this part" opens the operations view for its body region.
- Health details: the exact hit points of every body part next to its name (e.g. "Left leg 20/30"), and the time left for conditions whose label does not show it.
- "Operations planned: 2" in the health header when operations are planned – a click opens them.
- Safer drawing: a recipe without an icon shows a "?", a drawing error no longer leaves the tab with broken text or colours, and Esc in a search field just leaves the field.

0.4.1
- The silhouette (and the portrait) in the health tab always stands upright, also when the colonist lies wounded or downed.

0.4.0
- Operations in our own look (the "Operations" button in the health tab): planned operations on the left in their order – move up/down, pause, remove, and whether everything is at hand or what is missing; the best free doctor and the medical care below them; the silhouette in the middle; every possible operation on the right with search, kinds (implants & prostheses, remove, drugs, other) and body regions (head, torso, arms, legs, whole body). One click adds it – the game's own checks and confirmations stay.

0.3.0
- Health tab: the figure is now the colonist's own silhouette – of any race (HAR, xenotypes, mods) – coloured by the health of the body parts: green healthy, orange hurt, red badly hurt, dark for a missing limb. The marks sit on the silhouette. The button in the corner switches back to the normal portrait.

0.2.1
- Bills: long recipe names no longer run under the info button.

0.2.0
- Health tab: an overview on the left (state in one line – bleeding with time until death, a disease you are losing, needs tending –, medical care, self-tend, food policy, pain and capacities with bars), the colonist's whole figure in the middle with coloured marks where something is wrong (click a mark to see only that part), and the conditions grouped by body part on the right with chips (bleeds, tended, needs tending, permanent) and immunity against the disease as two bars.
- Operations and the game's own health tab are one click away; other health tab mods are switched off while ours is on (both can be changed in the settings).

0.1.3
- Bill priorities 1–10: 1 is done first, 10 last, 5 is normal. The bills of a workbench are kept in that order, and colonists go first to the workbench with the most urgent bill. Saved with the game.

0.1.2
- Info about the products: hover a recipe (or a bill's picture) for what it makes, its value, weight, armor or damage, work, materials and skill; the "i" button or a click on the bill's picture opens the game's info card.

0.1.0
