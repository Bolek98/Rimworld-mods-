1.0.1
- Unwavering loyalty: the days count the time the prisoner or slave already spent with you (also before the mod was added), and slaves are included.
- Slaves who are not unwavering can be made free colonists in the QoL window (Prisoners tab), also slaves from other factions.
- Deconstruct everything: ancient walls, ruins, wrecks and other things the game only lets you destroy by attacking can be deconstructed (not buildings of hostile factions).
