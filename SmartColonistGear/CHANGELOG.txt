Smart Colonist Gear 2.15.0
==========================
- New: newer technology is preferred – between similar weapons and armor, guns win over bows and flak or powered armor over plate (setting, on by default).
- New: work tools are not weapons – pickaxes, sickles, shovels and other tools that speed up work are never picked as a weapon, and a tool in hand is swapped for a real weapon (setting, on by default).
- New: guests use colony gear (both on by default, Temperature tab):
  - Quest guests you control get weapons and clothes like colonists.
  - Visitors and guests borrow warm colony clothes when it is cold, and put their own warm clothes away when it is hot.
  - Their own things go into their backpack, not into your storage. When they leave (already when they start leaving, not at the map edge) they put their own things back on and the colony's gear stays here.
- Temperature right away: when the outdoor temperature crosses your cold or heat threshold, colonists put on warm clothes or take off what is too warm at once – no waiting for the next round, the calm time or the "recently taken off" memory. For one hour after the change they finish dressing quickly, item after item.
- New: "Why" for every gear change. The colonist button of this mod now lists the last gear changes and the reason for each – e.g. "put on Flak vest – attack, armor on", "took in hand Assault rifle – better weapon (instead of Revolver)", "took off Parka – too hot".
- It also shows who made the change: this mod, the game (outfit policy), you (by hand) or another mod.
- New: other mods that change colonist gear are listed in Settings, Other tab – both mods that change how gear is chosen and mods that actually changed gear in your game (with a count). If colonists swap gear back and forth, you can see which mod to switch off for that part.

Smart Colonist Gear 2.14.0
==========================
- Smarter gear choices:
  - Gas masks only for colonists who are actually in toxic air (toxic gas, fallout, heavy pollution), not for the whole colony.
  - Rot stink no longer counts as toxic air.
  - Pollution counts only when a colonist is out in a polluted area, not when stepping on one polluted cell.
  - Never two gas masks at once – the weaker one goes back to storage.
  - A helmet is no longer swapped for a gas mask unless the colonist really is in toxic air.
  - After the danger the gas mask comes off and the helmet goes back on (it is no longer blocked for a day).
  - An armored helmet is preferred over a plain hat or cap when the weather does not call for warm headwear.
- Ideology-required apparel and gear you forced or locked are never touched.

Smart Colonist Gear 2.13.0
==========================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).
- New: "Recommended settings" switch at the top of the settings, on by default for new players. The mod sets itself up and just works.
- While it is on, the detailed settings are locked (you can still browse them). Bans stay editable.
- Turn it off to customize – values that differ from recommended get an orange CHANGED mark, click it to restore one value, or use "Reset all to recommended".
- Recommended values update themselves when a new version improves them.
- Bigger settings window.
- Existing settings are kept – an update never overwrites your values.

Smart Colonist Gear 2.12.0
==========================
- Fixed: colonists endlessly putting armor on and taking it off (threat and pollution state no longer flickers).
  - A threat now counts only after it lasts a few seconds and ends a while after the last hostile is gone.
  - Pollution under the colonist's feet no longer toggles hazard gear on its own.
  - Emergency gear needs more confirmation checks before it is removed.
- New: colonists complete the whole armor set instead of wearing only some pieces, and never swap a set piece for a worse one (gas mask is an exception).
- New: extra warm clothing is added only when it is not hot.
