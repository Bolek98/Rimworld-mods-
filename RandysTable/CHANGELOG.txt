=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
0.3.0
- Mad Dice Max brings 30 more events (60 in all), five for every face: nights under the stars, guests, Max's balm, cook/scholar/gardener/sower/tamer challenges, high-or-low and the mystery box, Max's swap, rain dance, market day, a stray herd; on hard days blight, fever, poison rain, the toll and a blinding sun; on mad days the swarm, the hum, the jackpot, a long night and iron dice. Rewards and threats follow the difficulty like the first 30.

0.2.2
- The storyteller is now called Mad Dice Max (Czech: Šílený kostkař Max).

0.2.1
- The Gambler's events follow the game's difficulty: on easier difficulties rewards are bigger and challenge goals lower, on harder ones the other way round; raids from his events follow the threat scale, and on difficulties without big threats he sends no raids at all. The event list shows the current factors.

0.2.0
- The Gambler's own events: after a roll, Dice the Gambler may bring one of 30 events that fit the face of the die – bets with choices (odd or even, roulette, all in, tax), challenges with a deadline and a reward (craftsman, hunter, miner, builder, quiet days, fortress…), situations for a few days (lucky hand, gold fever, sharp minds, unlucky day…) and twisted raids and weather. Chance per day and every single event can be set in the settings.

0.1.5
- Dice the Gambler: the heaviest threats – infestations, mechanoid clusters, crashed ship parts – come only on a mad day (6). Setting, on by default.

0.1.4
- Dice the Gambler throws a seven-sided die now: a 7 rolls off the table and he throws again a few hours later (an ordinary day until then).

0.1.3
- The dice also set the weather (1 and 4 fine, 5 the worst this place knows, 6 any weather – also from mods; 2 and 3 now and then a change), and quiet days (2, 3) often bring one extra event. Events come from vanilla and all mods.

0.1.2
- The dice roll always shows: a hard day (5) brings at least one threat and a mad day (6) at least two random events during the day; both days also bring events a bit more often.

0.1.1
- A generous day's gift (trader caravan, wanderer …) gets fresh details when it actually arrives instead of the ones from hours earlier, and is retried for a shorter time.

0.1.0
- First version: Dice the Gambler storyteller (daily roll with mood and odds alert) and the storyteller Diary.
