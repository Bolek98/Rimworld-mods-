=== Not on the Workshop yet (test versions) ===
1.9.2
- Plants from other mods whose icon is the red "missing texture" square (wrong icon path in that mod) get the picture they are drawn with on the map, so buttons such as "Where it grows" and "Copy" show the plant. Plants with no picture at all are named in the log with their mod.

1.9.1
- Crop growth sync (setting, off): crops in one grow zone that were sown within about a day of each other ripen together – the slower ones catch up a little every hour. A later sowing is left alone.

1.9.0
- "Where it grows": a button on every grow zone, hydroponics basin and planter shows on the map where that plant grows – green well, yellow slower, red not at all – and the reason under the mouse (too cold or hot now, poor soil, too dark under a roof, needs a roof, built over, only in hydroponics). Right click the button to check another plant. Checked a few thousand cells per frame, refreshed every few seconds.
- Plants from other mods: wild plants that no biome lists are added to the wild plant (and, when they grow in the cold, cold biome) groups of Hardy plants, and plants that give wood but are not marked as trees count as trees for speed, yield and grow days. Marked "auto" in the Plants tab with the reason; one switch to turn it off.

1.8.2
- Settings: a new "Bolek98's mods" button (next to "Report a bug") – all of Bolek98's mods you run in one list with their versions and settings, and saving or loading all their settings at once (for another PC or a reinstall).

1.8.1
- Settings: new "Report a bug" button (bottom left) – a short report for a Workshop comment and a full one for GitHub Issues, with this mod's errors, the mod list and other mods that change the same game code. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself.

=== Workshop release 1.8.0 (23 Oct 2026) – everything since the previous Workshop version ===

Evergrowth 1.7.0
================
- RimWorld 1.7 supported (tested on 1.7; still works on 1.6).
- New settings look: profile cards on top – Vanilla, Recommended, Lush, Custom.
- Recommended is the default for new players: faster growth, bigger harvests, no blight. It updates itself when a new version improves it.
- Changing anything switches the profile to Custom. Values that differ from recommended get an orange CHANGED mark – click it to restore the value.
- Bigger settings window, clearer sections (Growth, Yield and diseases, Temperatures, Which plants).
- Existing settings are kept – an update never overwrites your values.
